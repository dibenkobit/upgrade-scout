Finds the gear upgrades and consumables on the auction house that you can use and afford.

Open the auction house, click the Upgrades tab and press Scan. You get a list of gear that is better than what you wear, slot by slot, with prices. Press Buy on the one you want.

The Consumables tab does the same for potions, elixirs, flasks, food, bandages and oils. It lists the ones your character can use and scores what each one adds. Best for me keeps one of each kind: the one that gives you the most, at its lowest price.

Away from the auction house, the tooltip of any item shows how much better it is than what you wear: in your bags, in loot, in a quest reward and in a chat link.

What it does

  • The scan shows only what you can equip right now and can pay for.
  • The Consumables tab lists potions, elixirs, flasks, food, bandages and oils you can use, the most useful for your character first. What is of no use to you is left out.
  • Marks two items in every slot: the Best upgrade is the strongest, the Best value is the cheapest per point of improvement.
  • Best set for picks the upgrades that give the most together for your gold, or for a sum you type.
  • Shops for your other characters too. A button at the mailbox puts the gear you bought into a letter to them.
  • Any item's tooltip shows how much better it is than what you wear, for every spec of your class, such as Arms, Fury and Protection for a warrior.
  • A green arrow on the icon of an item in your bags or bank means it is an upgrade you can put on now. A grey one: an upgrade once you reach its level.
  • Works right after you install it. Nothing to set up: the Profile dropdown starts on a profile that suits your class.
  • Scores gear with its own stat weights for every talent tree, worked out with the wowsims/forever simulator. No other addon is needed.

Profiles

  • A profile says what each stat is worth for one talent tree. The addon starts on one that suits your class; pick yours in the Profile dropdown.
  • The weights are for a level 60 character, so they are an approximation. Below 60, hit, crit, haste and expertise count for less, as simulations of leveling characters show. Stamina (except for tanks), and a healer's Intellect, Spirit and mana regeneration, are set by hand. Armor, shield block, defense, dodge and parry count on every profile: against Stamina, as much as they help the simulator's tank. A ~ marks a profile whose weights are estimated because no simulation covers its tree yet.
  • /upscout stats and an item shows how the item gets its score, stat by stat.

Requirements

  • World of Warcraft Forever. No other addon.

Any class, any level. English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), German, Russian, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and Korean.
