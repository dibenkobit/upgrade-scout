Finds gear upgrades and consumables on the auction house that you can use and afford, scored by Pawn.

What you get

  • One scan checks every gear slot.
  • Shows only gear you can equip right now.
  • Hides what you cannot afford.
  • Marks the Best upgrade and the Best value in each slot.
  • Scores come from your Pawn scale.
  • Buyout prices, each checked against the real auctions.
  • Buy straight from the list.
  • A second tab lists the consumables worth the most to your character: what an elixir, a flask or a meal adds is scored on the same Pawn scale, and what your scale has no use for is left out.
  • Makes both lists for your other characters too: stand at the auction house on one character and see what is worth buying for another.
  • Works for any class and level.

How to use

  1. Open the auction house and click the Upgrades tab.
  2. Press Scan.
  3. Pick an item and press Buy.
  4. For potions and food, do the same on the Consumables tab.
  5. For another character of yours: log in to it once with the addon, then pick it in the Pawn scale dropdown of the character at the auction house.

Requirements

  • World of Warcraft Forever.
  • Pawn, installed and enabled.

Languages

English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Simplified Chinese.
