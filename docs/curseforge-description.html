Shows gear upgrades in every item tooltip and finds the ones you can afford at the auction house. Pawn is not needed.

In any tooltip

Point at an item in your bags, in loot, in a quest reward, in a chat link or at the auction house. The tooltip shows how much better the item is than what you wear, in percent, for every spec of your class, such as Arms, Fury and Protection for a warrior. Each spec is in the colour of your class.

At the auction house

Click the Upgrades tab and press Scan. You get a list of gear that is better than what you wear, slot by slot, with prices. Press Buy on the one you want.

What it does

  • Works right after you install it. There is one setting, the Profile dropdown, and it starts on a profile that suits your class.
  • Any item's tooltip shows how much better it is than what you wear, for every spec of your class.
  • The scan shows only what you can equip right now and can pay for.
  • Marks two items in every slot: the Best upgrade is the strongest, the Best value is the cheapest per point of improvement.
  • Best set for picks the upgrades that give the most together for your gold, or for a sum you type.
  • The Consumables tab lists potions, elixirs, food and bandages you can use, the most useful for your character first.
  • Shops for your other characters too. A button at the mailbox puts the gear you bought into a letter to them.
  • Scores gear with its own stat weights for every talent tree, worked out with the wowsims/forever simulator.

Profiles

  • A profile says what each stat is worth for one talent tree. The addon starts on one that suits your class; pick yours in the Profile dropdown. That is the only setting.
  • The weights are for a level 60 character, so they are an approximation. Below 60, hit, crit, haste and expertise count for less, as simulations of leveling characters show. Stamina (except for tanks), and a healer's Intellect, Spirit and mana regeneration, are set by hand. Armor, shield block, defense, dodge and parry count on every profile: against Stamina, as much as they help the simulator's tank. A ~ marks a profile whose weights are estimated because no simulation covers its tree yet.
  • /upscout stats and an item shows how the item gets its score, stat by stat.

Requirements

  • World of Warcraft Forever. No other addon.

Any class, any level. English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), German, Russian, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and Korean.
