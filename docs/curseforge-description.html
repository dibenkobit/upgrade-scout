Finds gear upgrades on the auction house that you can equip and afford.

Open the auction house, click the Upgrades tab and press Scan. You get a list of gear that is better than what you wear, slot by slot, with prices. Press Buy on the one you want.

What it does

  • Scores gear with Pawn, so the list matches Pawn's green arrows.
  • Shows only what you can equip right now and can pay for.
  • Marks the Best upgrade and the Best value in every slot.
  • Best set for picks the upgrades that give the most together for your gold, or for a sum you type.
  • The Consumables tab lists potions, elixirs, food and bandages you can use, the most useful for your character first.
  • Both tabs work for your other characters too. Log in to the other character once, then pick its scale in the Pawn scale dropdown. A button at the mailbox puts the gear you bought into a letter to it.

Requirements

  • World of Warcraft Forever.
  • Pawn, installed and enabled.

Any class, any level. English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.
