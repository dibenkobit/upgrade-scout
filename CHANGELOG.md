# Changelog

## 2.11.0

- **Forever Leveling BiS** can score your gear. In the addon's window, under **Who scores your
  gear?**, choose **Forever Leveling BiS**: the lists at the auction house then go by its scores,
  not by the addon's weights.
- Your character is scored for the main spec you chose in Forever Leveling BiS, with the numbers
  its own tooltip shows. To score for another spec, choose another main spec there: the lists
  follow. **Change in Forever Leveling BiS**, beside the spec, opens its settings.
- With Forever Leveling BiS chosen, its own tooltip lines say what an item is worth and its own
  arrows mark the upgrades in your bags, so the addon leaves its own out. Switch either off in
  its settings, and the addon's come back, by its scores. In the bank, where it draws no arrows,
  the addon's stay.
- A weapon is scored for the hand it would go in: the main hand and the off hand count
  differently, as they do in Forever Leveling BiS. A weapon it says your spec cannot use is not
  listed.
- A list made for another of your characters goes by Forever Leveling BiS's weights for that
  character's class, spec and level. The **Profile** dropdown lists the specs. The addon's lines
  say whose score it is: **Upgrade Scout (Forever Leveling BiS score)**.
- The choice is for the whole account. Without Forever Leveling BiS, or with it switched off, the
  addon scores by its own weights.
- A price check the auction house leaves without an answer is asked again after 3 seconds, not 8:
  the scan no longer seems stuck on it.
- The scoring no longer stands still for several seconds near its end. The list appears while
  the game is still loading the last few items, and those join it as they come.
- If you have Forever Leveling BiS, the window comes up once after the update to show you the
  choice.

## 2.10.1

- Prices that are not checked yet are no longer dimmed. A dimmed row looked like one you could
  not buy, and most of those prices are right.
- While the prices are checked, the status line says that some of them may be bids. The row's
  tooltip still says when its price is not checked yet.

## 2.10.0

- The list no longer waits for the prices. It appears as soon as the scan knows the items,
  each at the lowest price among its auctions, and the prices are checked after that.
- A price that is not checked yet is dimmed, and the row's tooltip says so. Once checked it
  turns bright or changes, or the row goes: the lowest price of an item can be a bid.
- You can buy while the prices are being checked. The item is looked up first, as before any
  purchase, and the box asks at its real price.

## 2.9.1

- With **Everything I can use** ticked, what adds to your character stands at the top of each
  heading, the highest score first. Food, drink and potions that only restore come after it.
  Before, they came first.
- The headings with something scored under them come first, and the ones with nothing scored
  after them. With everything listed, the list no longer opens with healing potions.

## 2.9.0

- The **Consumables** tab now lists only what adds to your character: the consumables that have
  a score on your profile. Before, it listed everything you could use, and the elixirs stood
  under rows of food, water and potions.
- **Everything I can use**, a new box in the **Filters** menu, brings the rest back: plain food
  and drink, healing and mana potions, bandages.
- When nothing for sale has a score, the list says so and names the box.

## 2.8.0

- **Forever Companion** can score your gear. In the addon's window, under **Who scores your
  gear?**, choose **Forever Companion**: the lists at the auction house then go by its gear
  scales, not by the addon's own weights. The **Profile** dropdown lists the scales, for your
  other characters too.
- The scale in use is the one Forever Companion has in use. Choose another in the **Profile**
  dropdown or in the addon's window, and it is chosen in Forever Companion as well.
- With Forever Companion chosen, its own line says what an item is worth and its own arrows
  mark the upgrades in your bags, so the addon leaves its own out. Switch either off in Forever
  Companion's options, and the addon's come back, on its scales.
- A list made for another of your characters is about that character. Forever Companion knows
  only the one you are playing, so in such a list the tooltip has the addon's lines, and
  neither Forever Companion's line nor its BiS lists. The addon's lines say whose score it is:
  **Upgrade Scout (Forever Companion score)**.
- The choice is for the whole account. Without Forever Companion, or with it switched off, the
  addon scores by its own weights as before.
- If you have Forever Companion, the window comes up once after the update to show you the
  choice.
- In a list for another of your characters, the game's own comparison no longer lies under
  the tooltip of what that character wears. It did with Shift held, or with the game set to
  always compare items.

## 2.7.0

- **Buyout up to**, a new filter on the Upgrades tab. Type a sum in gold and silver, and the
  list leaves out every item that costs more. The **Best upgrade** in each slot is then the
  best one for that price.
- It stands under **Only what I can afford** in the **Filters** menu. With both ticked, the
  smaller of the two limits counts.
- A scan with it on does not look up prices over the sum, so it takes less time.
- Each character has its own sum. When you shop for another character, you see and set that
  character's.
- **Best set for** has its own budget and does not use it.
- In Russian, the names of the filters beside the **Filters** field have a dot between them.
  Before, there was a square.

## 2.6.1

- **One-Hand** and **Two-Hand** stand in one column in a weapon slot. Before, the word moved
  to the right in the rows without a **Best upgrade** or **Best value** badge.
- **Best set for** no longer says that no upgrade fits the sum while a scan is still running.
- With Pawn chosen, the yellow `*` and **Has effects the score does not count** no longer stand
  on an item that has no effects. A line another addon adds to tooltips, an item's ID for one,
  was taken for an effect.

## 2.6.0

- **Pawn** can score your gear. In the addon's window, under **Who scores your gear?**, choose
  **Pawn**: the lists at the auction house and the bag arrows then go by your Pawn scales, not
  by the addon's own weights. The **Profile** dropdown lists the scales, for your other
  characters too.
- With Pawn chosen, Pawn's lines say what an item is worth, so the addon leaves its own out of
  the tooltip.
- A list made for another of your characters is about that character. Pawn knows only the one
  you are playing, so in such a list the tooltip has the addon's lines and none of Pawn's. They
  go by that character's Pawn scales and say so: **Upgrade Scout (Pawn score)**.
- The choice is for the whole account. Without Pawn, or with Pawn switched off, the addon
  scores by its own weights as before.
- If you have Pawn, the window comes up once after the update to show you the choice.
- The addon's window asks two things about item tooltips. How: **All specs** shows a line for
  every talent tree of your class, as before. **Only my spec** keeps one line: how much better
  the item is for the tree you play.
- Where: one box for the addon's tabs at the auction house, one for the rest of the game (bags,
  loot, chat). Before, the lines on the addon's tabs could not be switched off.
- A third box is for the notes in the tooltip of a row on the addon's tabs: how much gold you
  are short of, that an item takes both hands. It works with Pawn chosen too.
- The window has a new layout: the auction house and the bags side by side, and the tooltip in
  a block of its own under them.
- When an item's price has changed since the scan, **Buy** still asks: the box shows the
  **old price** and the **new price**, and you buy or cancel. Before, the chat said so and you
  had to press Buy again.
- If someone else buys the item while the box is up, the box stays. It looks the item up again
  and shows the next price, or says that the item is **no longer for sale**. Before, the box
  closed and you went back to the list.
- The box always shows the **net cost** of gear.
- A row on the Upgrades tab has two prices side by side: the **Buyout** and the **Net cost**.
  Before, **Net cost** was a tick box that swapped one for the other.
- That tick box is now **Value by net cost**. It only says which of the two prices **Best
  value** and **Top 5 per slot** go by: that one is white, the other grey. Ticking it no longer
  sends the list back to its top.
- A price always fits its column. A very large one drops its copper, then its silver; the box
  before a purchase has it in full.
- Coins stand closer together, as in your bags: in the lists, in the box before a purchase and
  in the gold display under the addon's tabs.
- **Best set for** always says what the set costs in the end, beside what you pay for it.
- A row's tooltip no longer has the lines for the buyout and the vendor's price: both prices
  are in the row.
- The item in the box before a purchase has the same tooltip as its row in the list. Before,
  the addon's lines were missing there.
- The tick boxes above the lists are now in one menu, **Filters**, at the right end of the strip.
  It stays open while you tick. The filters that are on are named beside it.
- **Best armor type for the class**, a new filter on the Upgrades tab. It leaves out armor
  lighter than the heaviest your character has learned to wear: a hunter is offered leather, and
  mail once it has learned mail. Cloaks, rings, weapons and shields stay. The bag arrows follow
  it.
- **Upgrade at least**, a new filter with a number. It leaves out upgrades smaller than that
  percent. An item for an empty slot always stays.
- Each character has its own two. When you shop for another character, you see and set that
  character's.

## 2.5.0

- A welcome window comes up on your first login. It shows what the addon puts into the game: the
  tabs at the auction house, the lines in item tooltips and the arrows in your bags.
- The tooltip lines and the bag arrows can be switched off in that window.
- A button at the minimap opens the window again. So does `/upscout`; at the auction house the
  command still goes to the Upgrades tab.
- The **Profile** starts on the talent tree you have the most points in, no longer on one fixed
  for your class. A profile you chose yourself stays.
- Ticking or unticking **Net cost** no longer sends for prices. The list is repriced on the
  spot: a scan now looks up what it needs either way.
- A scan leaves alone gear that is sold by the piece, like a grey necklace that stacks. One
  such item held every scan up, and the list then said that the scan had stopped early.
- A name in another alphabet than the game's language is written in letters, not in squares:
  a Russian character's name on an English client.
- When the auction house leaves a price check unanswered, the status line says how many.

## 2.4.0

- **Net cost**, a new box on the Upgrades tab. Ticked, the list prices gear at what it costs
  you in the end: the buyout less what a vendor pays for it when you are done with it. Bought
  for 4 silver and sold for 3, it cost you 1. **Best value** and **Top 5 per slot** go by that
  cost. What you can afford, and **Best set for**, still go by the buyout.
- The list of upgrades no longer shows the price per point. The buyout stands next to the
  **Buy** button, and item names have more room. **Best value** is picked as before.

## 2.3.1

## 2.3.0

- An arrow on the icon of an item in your bags or bank marks an upgrade on your profile:
  green when you can put it on now, grey when it asks for a higher level than yours.
- Buying gear asks in the same box as buying consumables, with the item's gain and buyout. The

- The strip at the mailbox comes up with the mail window every time. It lets go of what you send,

## 2.0.1

- Armor counts on every profile, not only a tank's, and so do shield block, defense, dodge and

  over better mail.

## 2.0.0

- Pawn is no longer needed. The addon scores gear with its own stat weights for every talent tree,
  worked out with the [wowsims/forever](https://github.com/wowsims/forever) simulator.
- One setting: the **Profile** dropdown. It starts on a profile that suits your class. The Pawn
  scale chosen in 1.x is replaced by it.
- Below level 60, hit, crit, haste and expertise count for less, as simulations of leveling
  characters show.
- Stamina (except for tanks), and a healer's Intellect, Spirit and mana regeneration, are set by hand: a simulation
  of damage cannot tell what they are worth.
- `/upscout stats` and an item shows how the item gets its score, stat by stat.
- The **Upgrade** column shows one number: how much better the item is, in percent. **New**
  stands for an empty slot, and points for a slot whose item adds nothing on your profile. The points are in the tooltip.
- Gear with a weapon skill or a bonus against one kind of creature gets the yellow `*`: the score
  does not count those.
- The addon's lines in an item's tooltip start with its name and the profile the scores are on.
- Fixed: two variants of one item with the same score, such as "of the Tiger" and "of the Whale",
  showed as one offer, and the second took the place of the first.
- Fixed: gear you bought counted as already worn when you wore another variant of the same item.
- Fixed: a consumable sold as separate auctions showed a part of its price, and the chat named the
  wrong number bought.
- Fixed: the box for buying a consumable stayed open with a grey button when the auction house
  turned the purchase down.
- Fixed: on a big auction house, **Best set for** could find nothing for a small sum.
- Fixed: the spell crit of oils and flasks was left out of their score.
- A second copy of a Unique-Equipped item you wear is no longer offered.
- For another character: gear that asks for a rank or a reputation is left out, in every language.
- Fixed: gear sent to another character could stay on the mailbox button after the letter went.

## 1.3.0

## 1.2.0

- **Best set for a budget**: tick **Best set for** under the list of upgrades. The list then shows
  the upgrades that give the most together for your gold, or for a smaller sum you type.
- **Mail to another character**: the addon remembers the gear you buy for another character. At a
  mailbox, a button puts it into a letter to that character. You check the letter and press Send.

## 1.1.0

- **Consumables** tab: potions, elixirs, food, bandages and other consumables you can use right
  now. Pawn scores what they add, the same way as gear. **Best for me** keeps the best of each kind.
- **For another character**: both lists can be made for another character of yours. Log in to it
  once with the addon, then pick its scale in the Pawn scale dropdown.
- A new look for the tab. Click a slot's heading to fold the slot.
- `/upscout forget Name` removes a deleted character from the dropdown.
- Fixed: prices such as 2g 90s 0c had a gap in them.

## 1.0.1

- Fixed: the status line always said "Scanned 1 Sec ago".

## 1.0.0

First release, for World of Warcraft Forever 1.60.1.

- **Upgrades** tab in the auction house: gear you can equip right now that is better than what you
  wear, slot by slot.
- Scores come from Pawn. Pawn is required.
- **Best upgrade** and **Best value** are marked in every slot.
- **Top 5 per slot** keeps only the options worth considering.
- **Only what I can afford** hides what costs more than you have.
- Buyout prices only, each checked against the real auctions.
- **Buy** from the list. The addon asks before paying.
- English, Simplified Chinese, German, Russian, French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
